# Single-stroke fonts

Generated by `scripts/build-stroke-fonts.ts` from the SVG fonts in the [hersheytext](https://github.com/techninja/hersheytextjs) package (MIT). The Hershey fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards; the format of the font data was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc. The EMS fonts by Evil Mad Scientist Laboratories are licensed under the SIL Open Font License (https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). Each font's own notice follows.
